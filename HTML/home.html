Undertale is a 2015 indie RPG created by Toby Fox, known for its unique combat system, deep storytelling, and memorable characters. Unlike traditional RPGs, players can befriend enemies instead of fighting them, leading to different outcomes based on their choices. The game follows a child who falls into the Underground, a world filled with monsters. Players can choose a Pacifist, Neutral, or Genocide route, each changing the story and how characters react. Undertale is praised for its emotional depth, humor, and unforgettable soundtrack, featuring songs like Megalovania.
